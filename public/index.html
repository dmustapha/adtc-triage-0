How it works

Three bounded review steps

1

Choose a workflow

Assess a pediatric IMCI or adult mhGAP case, or run an ordinary prompt through the local model.

2

Record what controls the result

For clinical review, record age and all seven structured observations. Missing values remain visibly not assessed.

3

Review the evidence

Results separate deterministic policy, supervised classification, WHO references, and local model provenance.

Why it is different

Built around explicit authority and evidence boundaries

Local product boundary

The product is served on loopback. Loopback binding alone is not presented as proof of completed offline inference.

Deterministic safety boundary

Structured observations own emergency and missing-assessment decisions. Broader classifications stay provisional until a trained worker confirms them.

Evidence before claims

Model residency, guideline readiness, egress proof, and request metrics remain absent until the running tool observes them.

Evidence appears only after a verified local run.

The canonical model identity is pinned, but this local checkout may have no resident model, an empty guideline store, and no measured egress proof. Historical or raw-profiler evidence is kept separate from product-runtime evidence.
